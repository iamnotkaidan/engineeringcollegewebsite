About Engineering College

Founded in 1998, Engineering College is a teaching-focused institution of about 2,400 students. We combine rigorous theory with hands-on practice, and every program ends with a capstone project solving a real problem for an employer or community.

Our campus has six laboratories, a fabrication workshop and a 24-hour study hall.

Programs

Civil Engineering

Structures, transport and water systems. Includes a field-survey semester and a bridge design studio.

4 years · BEng

Mechanical Engineering

Machines, materials and manufacturing with weekly workshop time and a final prototype project.

4 years · BEng

Electrical Engineering

Power systems, electronics and control, with a renewable-energy lab on campus.

4 years · BEng

Computer Engineering

Embedded systems, networks and software, built around team projects with local industry.

4 years · BEng

Software Engineering

Design, testing and delivery of reliable software, with a paid summer placement.

4 years · BEng

Engineering Management

A one-year master's for graduates who lead technical teams, budgets and projects.

1 year · MEng
