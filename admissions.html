Admissions

Requirements, steps to apply and tuition at a glance.

Admissions overview

We admit students on academic record, a short motivation statement and an interview. No entrance exam is required for undergraduate programs. Applicants need secondary school certificates with mathematics and physics.

How to apply

  1. Register online. Create your account on the registration page.
  2. Upload documents. Certificates, ID and a one-page motivation statement.
  3. Interview. A 20-minute conversation, in person or by video.
  4. Decision. You receive an offer by email within 10 working days.
  5. Confirm your place. Pay the deposit before 1 August.
